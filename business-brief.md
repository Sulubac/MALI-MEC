## About MALI-MEC

MALI-MEC is a multi-entity organization encompassing:

- **Techiftiin Institute** — professional training and upskilling programs
- **MEC** (Main Entity / Consulting) — strategic consulting and AI integration
- **Urban Beach** — lifestyle / event brand
- **École Champion** — coaching and performance academy
- **Advisory & Consulting arm** — B2B engagements, appels d'offres, institutional clients

## Target Audience

Primary: Entrepreneurs, coaches, consultants, trainers, and service providers who:

- Know AI is important but feel overwhelmed by the number of tools
- Cannot connect AI capabilities to concrete business revenue
- Need systems, not just skills
- Operate in French-speaking African and MENA markets

Secondary: Institutional clients and B2B buyers evaluating AI integration proposals.

## Core Promise

Help entrepreneurs and organizations build AI-powered business systems that create leverage, generate qualified leads, and produce measurable revenue — without needing a full technical team.

## Business Goals

1. Validate market demand for AI-powered training and consulting offers
2. Build content that attracts qualified prospects on YouTube and social media
3. Convert content viewers into leads via high-value downloadable assets
4. Upsell leads into workshops, programs, and premium consulting engagements
5. Create a reusable system that applies to each new idea, offer, or campaign

## Offer Ecosystem (Value Ladder)

```
Free YouTube content / Social posts
        ↓
Lead magnet (Revenue Agent Template / AI Business Toolkit)
        ↓
Workshop or online program (Techiftiin / École Champion)
        ↓
Premium AI integration consulting (MEC Advisory)
        ↓
Institutional proposals and B2B retainers
```

## Content Strategy

- Platform: YouTube (primary), LinkedIn, Instagram (secondary)
- Language: French (primary), Arabic (secondary), English (for international reach)
- Format: Under 15-minute practical demonstrations
- Style: Advanced but accessible — show real systems being built live
- Tone: Strategic, credible, entrepreneurial, results-oriented
