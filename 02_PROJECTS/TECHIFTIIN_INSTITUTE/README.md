## Overview

## Description

Techiftiin Institute is a planned AI and technology training center focused on practical, market-ready education. The institute will offer courses in AI, programming, automation, and digital skills.

## Objectives

1. Establish a recognized AI training institution
2. Develop practical curriculum aligned with market needs
3. Secure government and international funding
4. Build a network of qualified instructors
5. Launch and operate sustainably
